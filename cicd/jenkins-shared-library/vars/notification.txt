Sends build status notifications via Email and Google Chat.

Usage:
  notification.started(true, false)
  notification.success()
  notification.failure(false, true)
  notification.aborted()

Parameters:
  chat  - (boolean) Send Google Chat notification (default: true)
  email - (boolean) Send Email notification (default: true)

Methods:
  started(<params>) - Sends a notification that the build has started.
  success(<params>) - Sends a notification that the build succeeded.
  failure(<params>) - Sends a notification that the build failed.
  aborted(<params>) - Sends a notification that the build was aborted.
